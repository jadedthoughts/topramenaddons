TOP RAMEN PROCS
A supplemental Top Ramen tool for WoW Forever: it counts how often the gear you wear
actually procs, so Top Ramen Gear's scores can use real proc rates instead of a guess.

Type /trp (or /trprocs) to open the window. It runs on its own whenever you fight.
For every item with a "Chance on hit" or "when struck" effect it shows: how long you've
worn it in combat, the hits that could set it off, the procs it caused, procs per minute,
chance per hit, the procs-per-minute rate (PPM) that works out to, and what Top Ramen Gear
assumes now. Like the game, PPM is based on the weapon's printed speed, not on how fast
you actually swing: Cat and Bear Form swing fast but roll the weapon's own chance. Hover a row for the details. Fewer than 10 procs is "early".

Buttons (and slash commands):
   Logging on/off   /trp off, /trp on     pause or resume counting
   Share            /trp share            send your counts to your group (they need Top Ramen Procs)
   Export           /trp export           copy the whole log, to send in for better Gear scores
   Reset            /trp reset            clear everything (the button needs two clicks)
   /trp report      print it in chat (/trp report all also lists procs that aren't from gear)

How a proc is matched to an item: by its name (in the item's name or effect text), or by
elimination: if a proc keeps happening while you wear only one item with an effect, it's
that item. If one is listed as "not matched yet", swap a piece of gear for a fight and it
sorts itself out. Your own spells, rogue poisons, Windfury, Clearcasting and weapon
enchants are never counted.

It only reads the combat log and your worn items. It never changes, moves or uses anything.
